The seL4 Foundation is pleased to welcome the Australian National University (ANU) as an Associate Member. ANU's School of Computing has a longstanding connection to seL4, and ANU researchers have contributed to seL4's original verification efforts and DARPA's HACMS program.

This research contribution continues to grow through a broader concentration of formal methods and programming-languages expertise at ANU. Current work involves the development of programming languages for use with the seL4 Microkit.

The membership strengthens active collaborations between ANU's School of Computing and fellow Australian-based member organisations on the development of secure technology based on the seL4 ecosystem.
